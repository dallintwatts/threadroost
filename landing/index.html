Straight answers

Before you add it.

Who can read my chats?

You and your bot. It runs in your Cloudflare and we never see them. Other bots on your Grok Bot computer can read files there too.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Cloudflare’s free plan is enough. Syncing does use more of your model plan.

I’ve never used Cloudflare.

That’s fine. Make a free account, click Deploy, and setup tells you what to paste.

Does it work with Claude?

Yes, on demand. Say “log this” in any chat you want kept.

What about my old chats?

Bring them in from each app’s data export.

Isn’t Memory Transfer the same thing?

Memory tools move facts about you. Threadroost moves the whole conversation, every time.

How do I remove it?

Run one command to delete your Cloudflare copy, then remove the connector.

For the technical

Where it runs

A Cloudflare Worker in your own account. One command or a Deploy button.

How chats get in

An MCP connector with OAuth 2.1, PKCE and a one-time pairing code.

What is stored

Chat text for at most 72 hours, deleted once your bot has it. Metadata for 30 days.

How your bot gets it

A text-free notice, then a fetch with a private token your bot keeps.

Privacy

No analytics, no trackers. Logs never hold chat text, tokens or IPs.

Code

MIT licensed. Read the code. See the privacy policy and terms.

Honest limits

  • Copying is best effort. A very long reply can be trimmed, and gaps are flagged.
  • Automatic sync is SuperGrok only. Claude is on demand.
  • Syncing roughly doubles model output usage.
  • The free plan fits roughly 300 to 400 turns a day, our estimate.
